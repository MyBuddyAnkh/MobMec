Process

How FixMyRide works

The platform works like a request-and-accept system between customers and nearby mechanics or helpers.

01

Create a request

The customer enters their vehicle details, location, and problem description.

02

Nearby helpers are notified

Mechanics or helpers can view available jobs near them and decide whether to accept.

03

Service begins

Once accepted, the request status updates and the helper starts heading to the customer.

04

Complete the job

After the issue is resolved, the request can be marked as completed and reviewed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can non-mechanics accept requests?

For simple tasks like tire changes or battery boosts, approved helpers may be allowed. More technical repairs should be handled by qualified mechanics.

Does FixMyRide replace towing?

No. FixMyRide is mainly for roadside help and mobile mechanic support. If the car needs major repair, towing may still be required.

How are prices calculated?

Pricing can depend on service type, distance, time, urgency, and mechanic availability.

Can customers track their request?

Yes. In a full version, customers should be able to track request status in real time.
